How to apologize to a client for a delay

Late work happens to everyone. What the client remembers is how you handled it, so tell them early and keep it simple.

Tell them as soon as you know

Don't wait until the due date. Early news gives the client time to adjust.

Own it, don't over-explain

Say sorry once. Give a brief reason only if it helps, and never blame the client.

Hi Sam, I'm sorry, but the logo files will be late. I underestimated the time they need.

Give a new date you can keep

Pick a date with some extra room. Meeting a new date matters more than promising a fast one.

I'll send them by Friday at noon. I'll message you tomorrow with a quick progress update.

Make it up if you can

A small gesture helps, like an extra round of edits or a short discount. Only offer what you can really give.
