How to ask customers for a review

Reviews help new customers trust you. Most happy customers will leave one, but only if you ask and make it easy.

Ask at the right moment

The best time is right after a happy moment: the job is finished, the customer says thank you, or they praise your work. Don't wait a month.

Make it one tap

Send a direct link to your review page. If they have to search for it, most people give up.

Keep the message short

Say thanks first, then ask for one minute of their time.

Hi Priya! Thanks so much for choosing us today. If you enjoyed it, would you leave a short review here: [your link]? It helps a lot.

A more formal version

Hello Mr. Rao, thank you for your business. We would appreciate a short review of your experience: [your link]. It takes about a minute.

Don't do this

Don't pay for reviews or ask people to write fake ones. Many review sites ban this and it can hurt your business.

Don't send the same message again and again. One reminder is enough.
