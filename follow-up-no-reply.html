How to follow up when someone doesn't reply

No reply usually means busy, not no. A good follow-up is short, kind, and easy to answer.

Wait a few days first

For most work messages, wait 3 to 5 days. For something urgent, wait a day or two and say why it is urgent.

Make it easy to answer

Ask one question that needs only a short reply. Repeat the key detail so they don't have to scroll back.

Hi Sam, just checking in on the proposal I sent on Monday. Do you have any questions, or should I go ahead and book the start date?

The second follow-up

After about a week, add a new reason to reply, such as a deadline or a small change.

Hi Sam, following up once more on the proposal. I can hold the start date until Friday. Let me know if you'd like it.

Know when to stop

After two or three tries, send one last friendly message and then move on. You can always reach out again later.

Hi Sam, I'll assume now isn't the right time. If things change, I'd be happy to help. Wishing you the best!
