How to write a late payment reminder email

A good reminder email is short, clear, and calm. It tells the client what is owed, since when, and what to do next.

What every reminder needs

The invoice number and the amount.

The original due date, or how many days late it is.

One clear next step, such as a date to pay by.

How to pay, or a link to the invoice.

Subject lines that get opened

Use plain words: "Reminder: invoice #12 due" works better than a clever line. Put the invoice number in the subject so it is easy to find later.

Day 1 to 3: friendly

Hi Sam, a quick reminder that invoice #12 for $450 was due on the 5th. Could you let me know when I can expect payment? Thanks so much!

Day 7 to 14: professional

Hi Sam, invoice #12 for $450 is now 10 days overdue. Please pay by Friday. If there is a problem, tell me and we can sort it out.

Day 15 or more: final notice

Keep it short and factual. Do not use angry words. Check your contract before mentioning late fees or pausing work.

Hi Sam, this is a final reminder about invoice #12 for $450, now 20 days overdue. Please pay within 48 hours. After that I will have to pause further work.
